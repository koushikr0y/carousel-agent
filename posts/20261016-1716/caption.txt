Ever wondered how to render thousands of independent characters without killing your frame rate in Godot 4? 🧟

Recent benchmarks on GPU-driven rendering show that while pushing 2,500 animated zombies to the GPU is totally doable, the real hidden killer isn't the draw calls—it's the CPU readback.

When your CPU constantly needs to query data back from the GPU buffers, you hit a massive synchronization bottleneck. The fix? Keep your data entirely GPU-side using compute shaders, MultiMeshInstance3D, and custom uniform buffers.

Key Takeaways:
• Offload transform matrices to storage buffers
• Avoid CPU-GPU readbacks during active gameplay
• Leverage Godot 4's modern rendering pipelines
